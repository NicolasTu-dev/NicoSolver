Frequently asked questions

Before you join

Do I need to know game theory to use it?

No. Quick Mode is designed so you don't need to know what a range or GTO is to get a clear answer. Advanced does assume you already play with some regularity.

Can I upgrade from Advanced to Complete later?

Yes, anytime. Your saved hands and configurations stay the same.

Does it work without an internet connection?

The calculation runs on your computer, so it doesn't depend on an external server to solve a hand.

How long does it take to solve a hand?

Seconds, not minutes. You can choose between a fast, approximate answer or a more precise one if you have time to spare.
